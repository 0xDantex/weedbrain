Three rules

Nothing here is a metaphor. Each rule maps to one thing on the chain.

01

A buy throws him a joint

The size of the buy sets the length of the joint, on a log scale with a ceiling. Several burn at once and burn down on their own. Every buy also flicks a lighter that pushes back the nearest buzzkill, the only thing that stops them.

CurveBuy on the curve, or a v4 swap into the token after graduation
02

A sell brings a buzzkill

A cop, a mom, a fed or a priest walks in, picked by the transaction hash and not by me. If it reaches him it puts out his longest joint. A big sell puts out two.

CurveSell on the curve, or a v4 swap out of the token
03

The haze is the drawdown

The further the price sits below its high, the less smoke hangs in the room and the colder the light. Buzzkills that got through add to it and that part heals slowly.

1 - price / high, plus the debt of doused joints

Ten stages, six of them here

The stage comes from how much is burning and how thick the haze is. Between stages the frames blend, so he never jumps.

How to check it

His state is a pure function of the genesis seed, the ordered trade log and the step number. The corner of the live page prints a hash of that state. Anyone can recompute it.

LOCAL

Your browser reads the chain itself. No server is involved. Every viewer starts from the same block and the same seed, so the hashes should match, but each one reads the RPC on their own and nothing forces them to agree.

SYNC

A collector process writes the log to shared files and every viewer reads those. One log, one state, one hash for everyone.

DEMO

No token set. A seeded generator makes up the trades and a red band says so across the scene.

What this is not

  • No utility. He is something to watch, not something that pays.
  • No yield, no staking, no rewards.
  • No promise about the price. The scene follows the chart, it does not move it.
  • He does not die. At the bottom he is armed and waiting and the next buy starts to bring him back.
  • Not trustless. The state is computed from public trades and you can recompute it, but the collector and this site are run by me.
